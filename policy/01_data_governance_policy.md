# DATA GOVERNANCE POLICY
**[Organisation Name]** | Version 1.0 | Effective: [Date] | Review: Annual

---

## 1. Purpose

This Policy establishes **[Organisation Name]**'s commitment to managing its data assets responsibly, consistently, and in full compliance with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA 2023)**. It defines the governance structures, roles, responsibilities, and standards required to ensure that data is accurate, accessible, secure, and used appropriately.

---

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to **all data assets** owned, collected, processed, or shared by [Organisation Name], including:
- Customer and prospect data
- Operational and transactional data
- Financial and accounting records
- Supplier and partner data
- Employee and HR records
- Any personal data as defined under **Section 65 of the NDPA 2023**

---

## 3. Governance Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Data Owner** | Ultimate accountability for data assets in their domain; approves access policy changes; signs off annual policy review |
| **Data Steward** | Day-to-day metadata catalogue maintenance; lineage documentation; FAIR quality monitoring; data classification |
| **Data Analyst** | Consumes governed datasets for analysis; flags data quality issues to Data Steward |
| **Data Consumer** | Read access to approved datasets; compliance with this Policy as a condition of access |
| **System Administrator** | Technical management of governance infrastructure; no access to governed data content (separation of duties) |

---

## 4. Core Governance Principles

1. **Accountability** — Every data asset has a named Data Owner and Data Steward.
2. **Transparency** — Metadata for all data assets is registered in the central catalogue.
3. **Integrity** — Data lineage is documented for all significant transformations.
4. **Least Privilege** — Access is granted only to the minimum required for the user's role.
5. **FAIR Compliance** — All data assets are assessed against FAIR principles (target score ≥ 75/100).

---

## 5. NDPA 2023 Compliance Commitments

| NDPA Obligation | [Organisation Name] Commitment |
|---|---|
| Lawful basis for processing (s.25) | Personal data collected only with valid consent or legitimate interest documented in the Data Processing Register |
| Technical & organisational measures (s.24) | RBAC policy engine, TLS encryption, audit logging, and access controls implemented |
| Retention limitation (s.26) | Retention periods documented in the Data Classification Standard; automated alerts for expiry |
| Breach notification — 72 hours (s.40) | Data Breach Response Procedure triggered immediately; NITDA notified within 72 hours |
| Data subject rights (s.34–39) | All requests acknowledged within 5 days; fulfilled within 30 days |

---

## 6. Data Catalogue Requirement

All data assets must be **registered in the Metadata Catalogue** within **5 business days** of creation or acquisition. Registration requires completion of the mandatory baseline metadata fields as defined in the **Data Catalogue Registration Procedure (Template 3)**.

---

## 7. Review Schedule

This Policy shall be reviewed **annually** by the Data Owner and Data Steward, or immediately following:
- A personal data breach
- A material change in data processing activities
- An amendment to the NDPA 2023 or associated regulations
